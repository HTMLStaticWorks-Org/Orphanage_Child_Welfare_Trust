HEALTH & NUTRITION

A healthy mind and body is every child's birthright.

Many children arrive at our trust shelter malnourished or coping with past emotional distress. Our full-time healthcare wing restores their vitality.

In partnership with pediatric hospitals and child psychologists, we provide monthly health assessments, immunizations, balanced diet planning, and trauma-informed counseling.

  • ✓
    Daily Nutritious Meal Regimen Three hot home-cooked meals + evening protein milk and fresh seasonal fruit baskets.
  • ✓
    Routine Pediatric Screenings Bi-annual dental checkups, vision tests, and comprehensive developmental screenings.
  • ✓
    Trauma-Informed Therapy Professional play therapy, expression workshops, and one-on-one adolescent counseling.
  • ✓
    Emergency Medical Access 24/7 on-call medical staff and partnerships with top Chennai multi-specialty hospitals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our orphanage shelter, donations, and volunteer opportunities.

Admissions strictly follow the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act. Children are placed under our care through the Child Welfare Committee (CWC), local childline referrals, or authorized district authorities following formal safety audits and documentation.

Yes. Little Hearts Child Welfare Trust is a registered public charitable trust with valid 12A and 80G certifications. Every Indian citizen donation qualifies for a 50% tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, and 80G receipts are issued instantly.

To preserve our children's daily routine, emotional security, and privacy, visits are strictly arranged by prior appointment on weekends (10:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM). Please reach out via our Contact page at least 3 days in advance to schedule your visit.

A monthly sponsorship of ₹1,000 covers nutritious food, school tuition, uniforms, healthcare, and extracurriculars. Sponsors receive semi-annual academic report cards, health updates, and handwritten holiday cards created by the children while respecting full child privacy regulations.

We welcome volunteers for weekend academic tutoring, spoken English classes, arts and crafts, sports coaching, and medical checkups. All volunteers undergo a mandatory background screening and child safeguarding orientation before working with children.
