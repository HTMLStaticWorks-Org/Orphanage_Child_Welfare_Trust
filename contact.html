VISIT & IN-KIND GIFTS

Visiting guidelines & in-kind donation wish list.

We warmly welcome supporters who wish to celebrate birthdays, anniversaries, or festival meals with our children.

  • Meal Sponsorships: You can sponsor a special nutritious festive lunch or dinner for all 120 residential children.
  • Educational Supplies: Unused notebooks, geometry boxes, English storybooks, and school bags are always needed.
  • Grocery Drop-Offs: Rice sacks, lentils, cooking oil, ghee, and fresh fruits can be delivered to our Anna Nagar pantry.
  • Child Privacy Protocol: To protect our children's emotional dignity, personal photography or recording during visits is strictly regulated.
