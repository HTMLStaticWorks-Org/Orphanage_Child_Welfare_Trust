FOUR PILLARS OF CHILD WELFARE

Comprehensive support, every step of the journey

We combine unconditional care with systematic programs designed to break generational poverty.

01 / PROTECTION

Safe Shelter & Loving Homes

We provide stable, clean residential homes, certified housemothers, strict child safeguarding protocols, and trusted adults who help children feel entirely safe and cherished.

02 / EDUCATION

Quality Schooling & Digital Skills

From English medium school tuition and STEM lab equipment to evening study circles and career guidance, we ensure academic excellence and curiosity thrive.

03 / WELLBEING

Nutritious Food & Healthcare

We connect children with balanced diets, routine pediatric visits, immunizations, play therapy, and emotional resilience workshops to nurture body and mind.

04 / VOCATIONAL

Life Skills & Youth Independence

Older youth participate in our creative arts, candle making, tailoring, and computer workshops, preparing them for higher studies and independent careers.

OUR CARE PATHWAY

From crisis intake to confident, independent adulthood.

Every orphan or vulnerable child who enters our shelter embarks on a structured 4-stage journey to healing and self-sufficiency.

  • Stage 1: Safe Intake & Medical Stabilization: Thorough medical screening, trauma-informed care, and legal guardianship documentation.
  • Stage 2: Foundation & Schooling: Immediate school enrollment, dedicated housemother attachment, and peer integration.
  • Stage 3: Talent & Vocational Discovery: Extracurricular sports, creative arts, computer programming, and vocational craft skills.
  • Stage 4: College Transition & Aftercare: University sponsorships, career mentorship, hostel assistance, and first job placement.
