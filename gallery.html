SAFEGUARDING & PRIVACY

Ethical storytelling and child protection policy.

At Little Hearts Child Welfare Trust, the dignity, emotional wellbeing, and safety of every child is our highest ethical priority.

  • ✓
    Informed Consent All photographs are published with guardian consent in compliance with the Juvenile Justice Act.
  • ✓
    Privacy First Real identities and sensitive past histories of protected children are strictly shielded.
  • ✓
    Visitor Guidelines Center visits are strictly appointment-based with mandatory identification and safeguarding orientation.
  • ✓
    Zero Commercial Exploitation We uphold empowering, dignified representations of our children at all times.
