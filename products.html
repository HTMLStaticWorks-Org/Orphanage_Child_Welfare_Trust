VOCATIONAL PURPOSE

Dignity, practical skills, and a brighter tomorrow.

When youth turn 18, our vocational programs ensure they step into the world equipped with financial literacy, practical trades, and immense confidence.

  • 100% Reinvested in Children: Every rupee earned directly funds food, healthcare, and higher education scholarships.
  • Personal Savings Account: A percentage of product sales goes directly into a personal savings trust fund for each youth artisan.
  • Therapeutic Value: Art, candle-making, and pottery provide soothing emotional expression and trauma relief.
  • Corporate & Event Orders: We provide customized gift hampers with personalized trust thank-you cards for weddings and corporate festivals.
